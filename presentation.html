Challenges in Manual Grievance Maintenance

Hidden Old Cases

Paper registers bury older complaints under new entries, causing delay in resolution.

❓

No Citizen Visibility

Citizens cannot track grievance status, assigned officers, or expected resolution timelines.

📊

Lack of Bottleneck Metrics

No analytical tracking for department response times or officer workloads.

Constraints & Integrity

Foreign Keys, CHECK constraints (priority 1–5, affected count ≥ 1), UNIQUE department/ward names, and future-date triggers.

Analytical SQL Queries

10 queries for Open cases, Pending > 15 days, Department metrics, Ward summaries, Officer workload, and Average resolution times.

Performance Analytics

Derived Metric Calculations & Formulas

Days Pending (Open & In Progress)

Days Pending = Today - Date Filed

Identifies long-waiting grievances requiring urgent dispatch.

Resolution Days (Resolved Cases)

Resolution Days = Date Resolved - Date Filed

Measures actual turnaround time for completed complaints.

Average Resolution Time Metric

Avg Resolution Time = Σ (Resolution Days) / Total Resolved

Calculates overall portal efficiency and department SLA performance.
